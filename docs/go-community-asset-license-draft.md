# GO community asset licence — draft for review

**Draft only — not approved, published, or legal advice.** GO and qualified counsel should review the legal wording, contributor authority, and outstanding decisions before this is used as an agreement. The pack's existing licence and contributor rights remain in force unless the rights holder validly agrees otherwise.

## Member practice licence: intended business terms

1. GO's stated policy is that community asset packs remain free forever for active GO Community members, with no additional asset-pack charge for practice access. Access to source files is limited to eligible active members; cancellation paid through the end of a period follows the membership entitlement rules. A separate commercial licence may be offered for a fee. Counsel should translate the perpetual pricing commitment into appropriate terms.
2. The practice licence permits **noncommercial game-development practice and prototypes only**. It does not authorize commercial use, sale, monetization, advertising-supported distribution, commercial client work, or use in a commercial game. The final agreement must define commercial use and borderline cases.
3. Members may incorporate assets into a noncommercial prototype for practice. GO intends these assets to be replaced with the member's own assets before a finished game is released. The final agreement must distinguish public practice prototypes from finished games and specify the replacement deadline.
4. When membership ends, access to source files ends and the member must not start new projects using the assets under the membership licence. Existing noncommercial projects created while eligible may remain available. What maintenance, updates, or retention of working copies are allowed after expiry remains to be decided.
5. Members must not share source files or a Drive access link with nonmembers, redistribute standalone or editable assets, or sublicense them. Incorporation into an eligible noncommercial prototype is subject to the final licence terms and third-party rights.
6. Commercial use requires a separate, express paid licence from the party authorized to grant it. GO intends to ask users who lack that licence to remove or replace the assets in a commercial game. A payment or a membership does not by itself create a commercial licence. A separate purchase flow, pricing, scope, refunds and delivery rules have not yet been specified.
7. GO records the creator's contribution in their GameDev Passport and awards an official contribution badge on publication. The final agreement must decide whether and how attribution is also required in projects or public releases.

## Contributor agreement: terms still required

GO needs permission from each relevant rights holder to host and distribute the submitted files under the member practice licence, including rights to handle derivatives and third-party dependencies as applicable. A proposed commercial resale requires a **separate explicit grant** from the rights holder, including any revenue share or compensation, scope, duration, withdrawal and existing-customer treatment. A simple statement that the contributor created the work is not enough to assume those commercial rights.

The final contributor agreement should cover ownership, rights and authority, licences already granted, use of third-party content, permitted sub-licensing, attribution, safety review, takedowns and withdrawal, warranties, liability, territory, governing law, and what happens to existing member or commercial licences. GO must retain the agreement version, identity, acceptance timestamp and the specific pack/version covered.

## Required acceptance and release controls

- Show the exact versioned member terms before access and record a separate acceptance for each applicable version and pack. Do not ask users to accept a draft.
- Record and review the contributor agreement separately. Publication must not silently relicense an existing CC or otherwise licensed pack.
- Verify provenance, manifest, licence, commercial rights, storage privacy and test access before approving a pack. Award one contribution badge per published pack and retain creator credit.
- The current Drive redirects do not enforce these terms or remove access to already downloaded files. Private storage or verified Drive permissions with grant/revocation reconciliation are prerequisites for offering this as subscription-only file access.

## Outstanding decisions

- Which verified Google account receives a member's Drive access if it differs from the GO billing email.
- Commercial licence prices and terms for specific packs, whether a store is enabled, contributor consent and payout arrangements, and refund/revocation policy.
- Definition of commercial use; allowed post-expiry maintenance and updates; mandatory attribution in projects; contractor/team use; AI training; notices, enforcement, governing law and dispute process.
- How verified prior copies and historic public or CC licences are handled without promising rights GO cannot revoke.
