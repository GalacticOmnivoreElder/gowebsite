# GO community timeline research and draft entries

Research snapshot: 30 October 2026. This is a working document, not published timeline content. Dates are exact only where the cited source supports them. A report's publication date is not necessarily the date the activity occurred. The public timeline was empty when checked. Private Drive material must not be linked publicly or used for a project entry until the owner approves the specific project and wording.

## Public-source entries prepared for publication

### 20 April 2023 — Game Development Workshop #2
**Category:** event · **State:** completed

GO's second game-development workshop was scheduled at Hacklab KIKA in Skopje. A later interview describes Kosta Markovski sharing the idea for *Life of Slaviyan* during the workshop, where the group built on it together. Hacklab KIKA was the venue, not a part of GO.

Sources: [KIKA event listing](https://kika.spodeli.org/event/game-development-workshop-2-galactic-omnivore/); [October 2023 interview](https://popup.mk/ivan-kikerkov-galactic-omnivore-zarazi-se-so-entuzijazmot-na-ovaa-egalitarna-gejmerska-zaednicza/).

### 24–28 January 2024 — Online Global Game Jam site
**Timeline date:** 24 January 2024 · **Category:** event · **State:** completed

GO organized an online Global Game Jam site for participants in and around North Macedonia. The official site records the dates and lists games submitted at the site. This was a GO-organized site within the wider Global Game Jam, not ownership of the international event.

Source: [Global Game Jam site](https://globalgamejam.org/jam-sites/2024/intergalactic).

### 31 May 2024 — Regional meeting in Skopje
**Category:** partnership · **State:** completed

GO representative Ivan Kikerkov attended a regional discussion with representatives of MAGDA and the Croatian Game Development Alliance (CGDA). CGDA's report, published 1 June, says the meeting took place the previous day and describes discussions on regional collaboration. The organizations remain separate.

Source: [CGDA's report](https://cgda.eu/en/cgda-and-magda-regional-meeting/).

### October–November 2024 — Game-development education and regional jam
**Timeline date:** 19 October 2024 · **Category:** event · **State:** completed

GO's *Empowering the Next Generation of Game Developers* combined practical educational modules with a regional game jam. Base42's published programme scheduled the jam to begin on 19 October, and GO's April 2025 community update reports on the programme in retrospect. GO's project listing describes the work as part of USAID's Youth Actively Create Opportunities activity, implemented by IESC with Youth Educational Forum and CEED Hub Skopje.

Sources: [Base42 programme](https://42.mk/events/galactic-omnivore-empowering-the-next-generation-of-game-developers); [GO's jam page](https://itch.io/jam/gogamejam2024); [GO community update](https://www.galacticomnivore.com/blog/community-update-april-2025); [GO portfolio](https://www.galacticomnivore.com/projects). The jam page lists 11 entries and describes the six modules as completed.

Date caution: the GO project listing gives a June 2024 end date, whereas the Base42 programme and retrospective describe October–November activities. Confirm whether these refer to separate phases before publishing an end date or combining records.

### 10 December 2024 — Navigator launched
**Category:** project · **State:** completed milestone

GO's retrospective says the human-rights educational game *Navigator* launched to mark International Human Rights Day, in partnership with Europe House and the Macedonian Young Lawyers Association. Describe the organizations as collaborators and the game as a project milestone, not proof of ownership by any one partner.

Source: [GO's Navigator retrospective](https://www.galacticomnivore.com/blog/navigator-1-0-50000-players-explored-the-galaxy-of-human-rights).

### 29 March 2025 — Glagolitic VR prototypes and open call reported
**Category:** project · **State:** in progress

GO reported functional prototypes for a Glagolitic-script VR project initiated by Prof. Laze Tripkov in collaboration with GO, and opened a call for creators to contribute to a further phase. The date is the article publication date, not a claimed project start or exhibition completion date.

Source: [GO Signal article](https://www.galacticomnivore.com/blog/macedonian-glagolitic-in-vr-immersive-letter-environments).

### 15 April 2025 — GO reports platform and community work
**Category:** milestone · **State:** completed milestone

In a community update, GO reported its platform launch, early community-made assets and a recap of educational and game-jam work. Use the article's publication date as the date of the report; it does not establish an exact platform launch date. The article's displayed title says April 2024, while its metadata and URL indicate April 2025; the discrepancy should be noted during review.

Source: [GO community update](https://www.galacticomnivore.com/blog/community-update-april-2025).

### 20 August 2025 — Statute adoption recorded
**Category:** organization · **State:** completed

The consolidated GO Statute states that it was adopted at an assembly held on 20 August 2025. It describes GO as an independent, nonpartisan nonprofit association and sets out objectives including practical game-development skills, collaboration, education and projects. This is a statute milestone; it does not establish the date of GO's original initiative or legal registration.

Source: [GO Statute](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1DRFhgeRC7GwwnC5u2W1IJOBc8SgOSxIm/view).

### 3 October 2025 — Battle Toynks core-loop update
**Category:** project · **State:** in progress

GO's development log reported an internally playable core loop for *Battle Toynks*, including tank movement, shooting, objectives and a win condition. The game remained in development; this was not a full commercial release.

Source: [Battle Toynks development log](https://galactic-omnivore.itch.io/battle-toynks/devlog/1065084/-dev-log-1-core-systems-are-online).

### 1–26 December 2025 — Passion to Profession Game Jam
**Timeline date:** 1 December 2025 · **Category:** event · **State:** completed

The Passion to Profession Game Jam brought creators together around games for good and received seven entries. MUGI and Semos Education organized it in collaboration with GO and Base42; GO hosted the itch.io jam page. This was a collaborative programme, not an exclusively GO-owned initiative.

Sources: [completed itch.io jam and submissions](https://itch.io/jam/passion-to-profession); [MUGI programme announcement](https://mugi.mk/post/69319eba6a4e1abf3e397e74).

### 16 June 2026 — PrintNPlay's Unwelcome Guests demo reported
**Category:** project · **State:** completed milestone

GO reported that a free *Unwelcome Guests* demo by PrintNPlay was available on Steam. The article describes PrintNPlay as a team conceived within GO's community and credits DOSMan Games as publisher. The article date is the report date, not a verified Steam release date.

Source: [GO Signal article](https://www.galacticomnivore.com/blog/from-the-go-community-to-steam-play-the-unwelcome-guests-demo).

### 16 July 2026 — Fourth Game Dev Meetup recap
**Category:** event · **State:** completed

GO reported that its fourth Game Dev Meetup brought creators together at Base42 to play locally developed games, meet developers and exchange feedback. Use 16 July as the report date until the event's actual date is verified. Base42 was the host venue, a separate organization.

Source: [GO Signal recap](https://www.galacticomnivore.com/blog/game-dev-meetup-4-a-growing-community-of-local-game-developers).
