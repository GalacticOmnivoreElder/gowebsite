## Our pitch

Galactic Omnivore is a nonprofit game-development community and platform rooted in Skopje, North Macedonia. Our purpose is to help creators connect learning with practical collaboration and visible work.

A creator needs more than a tutorial: people to collaborate with, useful feedback, clear project roles and opportunities to show progress. GO brings these steps into a shared journey: learn a skill, apply it to a project, collaborate with clear expectations, share the result and prepare the next playable milestone.

We welcome students, hobbyists, emerging creators and independent teams. GOHQ provides a physical meeting place alongside the online community. Mentorship, educational activities, community events, project visibility and publishing preparation support the journey where available.

Members contribute knowledge, time and creative work. Partners can support workshops, tools, mentorship and community projects. Membership and suitable professional services help sustain this work. Current membership and service terms are published on the website.

Our ambition is to help more creators demonstrate their skills, build dependable working relationships and move projects toward useful outcomes. This public edition adapts the mission and creator journey from the April 2025 GO Platform Pitch Deck Content. It replaces historical traction claims with dated evidence. The earlier pitch's forecasts, competitive assertions, proposed prices, fundraising asks and growth targets are not presented as current facts or offers.

## Calendar method

Sources: GO Core Team, GO Meetings, GO Events and GO Weekly Community Meetings. The reporting interval begins at 2023-10-17T00:00:00+02:00 and ends, exclusively, at 2026-10-17T00:00:00+02:00. All four searches returned a terminal page.

The four calendars returned 944 records. Matching event identities and matching normalized title/start/end across calendars identify 40 duplicate copies, leaving 904 unique entries. Repeated events on different dates remain separate occurrences.

The conservative headline count contains 263 confirmed, timed, clearly named meetings or appointments. Title rules identify meetings, calls, mentoring, consultations, interviews, stand-ups, sprint planning, coordination and similar explicit meeting terms. Office blocks, availability, reminders, declined invitations, all-day or undated events are excluded. Another 217 ambiguously titled records remain uncounted pending review. The remaining 424 entries are other exclusions.

## Evidence gaps and next additions

1. **Origin:** original founding act and registry documentation, with distinct dates for the initiative, association and platform launch.
2. **Calendar:** earlier calendars, review of the 217 ambiguous entries, duplicate corrections and evidence of attendance. Do not equate shared calendar entries with the founder's personal calls.
3. **Tasks:** a current export with issue key, type, status, resolution, resolution date and project. Reconcile duplicates and reopened issues before extending the totals.
4. **Projects:** a reviewed register with unique ID, public title, start date, completion date, deliverable, public release link and status. Map renamed projects and milestones to their parent project.
5. **Website usage:** verified analytics for visitors, active members, registrations and conversions, with bot/staff exclusions and date definitions. Calendar and Jira figures cannot substitute for these.
6. **Updates:** label each metric with its own source date, retain missing values as unknown, and update this report when new evidence changes a figure.

## Publication and maintenance

Public material contains aggregate figures and already-public project names. It omits calendar participants, email addresses, meeting links, task descriptions, comments and private notes.

The KPI snapshot is intentionally dated. The website does not read private calendars or internal trackers in visitors' browsers. New evidence should be reconciled privately, then the aggregate snapshot and this report updated together.
