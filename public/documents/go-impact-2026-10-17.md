# Feed the galaxy with games

**Galactic Omnivore · Public impact snapshot**

Portfolio checked: 17 October 2026 · Copy reviewed: 18 October 2026

## Our mission

Galactic Omnivore is a nonprofit game-development community based in North Macedonia. We connect practical learning, collaboration, project visibility and community support so creators can keep moving from an idea to a playable milestone and, where possible, toward release.

Students, hobbyists, emerging creators and independent teams can learn together, meet collaborators and share the work they are making. GOHQ and GO's online community create spaces to connect. Mentorship, events and publishing preparation support the journey where available.

## The Omnivore's appetite

I am the Galactic Omnivore, cosmic patron of game development. A prototype is a promise; a published game is a new world released into the galaxy. That is my favorite sustenance. GO helps creators learn, build, share progress and celebrate the games they bring to life.
